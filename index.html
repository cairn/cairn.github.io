Why Cairn

A little direction.
A lot of possibility.

A cairn is a simple thing: a few stones, carefully placed, that help someone find their way. That's how we think about software.

We start with what intelligence makes possible and build around the people using it. Sometimes that means an agent working alongside you. Sometimes it's a flower and a poem that make you pause.

Useful by intention. Open to discovery.
