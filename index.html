In the workspace

Less handoff.
More making.

Cairn is a terminal-based coding agent. It brings the agent into your development workflow: it explores your project, makes focused edits, and checks the result with your own tools, from first read to verified change.

macOS and Linuxcurl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cairn/cairn/main/install.sh | sh
Windows, PowerShellirm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cairn/cairn/main/install.ps1 | iex

Then run cairn --version to check it worked. Prefer a manual download? Binaries for macOS, Linux, and Windows are attached to each release, with checksums alongside. Stuck or curious? Ask in our Discord.

Why Cairn

A little direction.
A lot of possibility.

A cairn is a simple thing: a few stones, carefully placed, that help someone find their way. That is how we think about software.

We start with what intelligence makes possible and build around the people using it. Sometimes that means an agent working alongside you. Sometimes it is a flower and a poem that make you pause.

Useful by intention. Open to discovery. Care is our moat.
